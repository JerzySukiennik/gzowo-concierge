---
name: research
description: How to research current facts on the web: prices, opening hours, news, comparisons, how-to.
---
- Use web_search for anything that can change (prices, hours, news, availability, schedules, product versions). Never answer those from memory.
- Ask the search tool full, specific questions, include the city or year when relevant. If the first result is vague or contradictory, search once more with a sharper query (max 3 searches per question).
- Answer: conclusion first in one or two sentences, then the key numbers or facts. Mention where it comes from by site name when sources are returned, and say how fresh it is when the date matters.
- If sources disagree, say so and give the most reliable one. If nothing solid was found, say that instead of guessing.
- Dates: compare with today. Say "jutro", "od jutra", the weekday, or "już trwa", never "teraz" for something upcoming.
- Recommendations: pick one and justify in one line.
