2. Find real free blocks. School ends around the last lesson event; do not invent lessons or fixed meals he did not mention.
3. Reply with a short list: events with times, then free blocks worth using and one concrete suggestion for each (study, rest, training), prioritizing anything with a deadline or test in the next 3 days.
4. Keep it under about ten lines. Do not write anything to the calendar until he says so; offer once at the end ("wpisać do kalendarza?").
5. Remember stable routines he tells you (training days, bedtime) with remember so next plans fit them.
